Somali Canjeero

Canjeero is a traditional Somali food. It is soft and delicious.

What You Need

  • Flour
  • Water
  • Baking powder
  • Sugar
  • Salt

How to Make It

  1. Put the flour in a bowl.
  2. Add water, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
  3. Mix everything well.
  4. Heat a pan.
  5. Pour the mixture into the pan.
  6. Cook until ready.

Enjoy your meal!
